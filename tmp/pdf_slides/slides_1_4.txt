INTRODUCTION: DELHI AQI FORECASTING AND EARLY
                                  WARNING

      · Delhi's air quality can differ greatly from one monitoring station to another, so a single city-level
          value may not reflect local exposure.

      · Timely prediction of AQI can help identify possible high-pollution periods before they occur.

      · This project uses records from 30 Delhi monitoring stations and considers key pollutants such
          as PM2.5, PM10, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, sulphur dioxide, carbon monoxide and benzene.

      · A uniform pollutant dataset from 2017 to 2025 is used to calculate AQI consistently across the
          study period.

      · The forecasting model predicts station-level AQI for the next 1, 6, 12 and 24 hours.

      · The dashboard shows AQI category, pollutant concentrations, likely health hazards and early-
          warning information for the selected station and date.
